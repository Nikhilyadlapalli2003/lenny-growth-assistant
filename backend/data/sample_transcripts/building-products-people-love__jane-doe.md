We learned that growth loops matter more than one-off viral moments. A single retained habit,
reinforced weekly, compounded into more durable growth than any single viral spike we ever had.

When we redesigned onboarding, we moved from a static multi-field form to an interactive checklist
that showed progress in real time. Activation went up roughly 3x within the first month, because
users could see exactly how close they were to their first "aha" moment instead of guessing.

The biggest mistake early-stage teams make is optimizing top-of-funnel acquisition before they've
proven a single retention loop. We spent our first six months exclusively on retention cohorts
before touching paid acquisition at all.
