Most B2B SaaS teams underprice their first tier because they're afraid of losing deals. We found
the opposite: raising our entry price by 40% barely moved conversion, but doubled expansion revenue
within two quarters, because it repositioned the product as a serious tool rather than a toy.

Packaging should map to a customer's growth stage, not to feature count. We rebuilt our tiers around
"how many teammates are collaborating," not "how many projects you can create," and churn on the
mid-tier dropped by a third.
